When do the clocks change in the United Kingdom?

The clocks go back one hour on Sunday 25 October 2026 at 2:00 AM, which becomes 1:00 AM (GMT). Daylight saving time ends; you get an extra hour in bed.

What to do on the day

The change happens at 2:00 AM on the night of 24 October 2026 to 25 October 2026; almost everyone is asleep, which is the point. Anything with a network clock moves by itself: phones, laptops, smart speakers, most cars. Ovens, microwaves, wall clocks, older cars and some alarm clocks do not.

Going back is the easy one: an alarm set for 7:00 AM still rings at 7:00 AM, you simply get an extra hour before it. The sleep calculator works out the bedtime for the new wake time, and the 7:00 AM alarm page shows the bedtimes that fit full sleep cycles.

Calls across borders are the usual casualty. The United Kingdom and a place that changes on a different date, or not at all, are one hour apart from their normal difference for a few weeks: the time zone converter and meeting planner always use the offsets for the date you pick.

The United Kingdom uses UK (Europe/London). Major cities include London, Manchester, Birmingham, Edinburgh. See the current time in the United Kingdom for the live difference from your own clock.

Questions

Will the alarm ring if I close the tab or the laptop?

No. No website can ring after the browser is closed or the computer sleeps. Keep this tab open, plug in, and the clock keeps the screen awake for you. The Ready-to-ring check under the clock shows what is set up correctly.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes on Android while the tab is open (behind other tabs is fine). On iPhone the page must stay in the foreground because Safari does not let web pages play sound from the background. Add it to the Home Screen for a full-screen bedside clock.

Is it loud enough for heavy sleepers?

Pick Loud buzzer or Siren, set the volume slider to full, and switch on the sums-to-stop option in Settings so the alarm cannot be dismissed half asleep. The sunrise light brightens the screen before the alarm too.

Can I use my own sound?

Yes. In Settings, choose an MP3, WAV or OGG file. It is stored in this browser only and never uploaded. There are also 60 built-in sounds, from a twin-bell alarm clock and a rooster to an air horn and a music box, on the sounds page.

What do the sleep sounds do?

Rain, brown noise, white noise, fan and ocean are generated in the browser and play until the alarm, then fade out as it rings. They keep the tab active through the night.

Does it need an internet connection?

Only to load the page the first time. Alarms, sounds, the sleep calculator and the sunrise math all run on your device, and the page works offline once it has been opened.

Is anything stored or sent?

Alarms and settings are saved in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is no account.

Do the clocks go back or forward in October in the United Kingdom?

Back. At 2:00 AM on Sunday 25 October 2026 the clocks become 1:00 AM.

Will my phone alarm still ring at the right time?

Yes. Phones take the new time from the network and alarms are stored as wall-clock times, so a 7:00 AM alarm rings at 7:00 AM in the new time. The same applies to alarms set on this site: they follow your device clock.

Is the date the same every year in the United Kingdom?

The rule is fixed (for example the last Sunday of a month or the first Sunday of another), so the weekday is the same and the date moves within a week. The table above shows the exact dates for this year and next.
