About a 6 hours 20 minutes alarm

6 hours 20 minutes is about 4.2 sleep cycles of 90 minutes. If you want to wake at the end of a cycle, the alarms below at 6 hours and 7 hours 30 minutes are the closest cycle boundaries.

If you fall asleep in the usual 14 minutes, 6 hours 20 minutes gives about 6 hours 6 minutes of sleep, roughly 4.1 sleep cycles of 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle feels better than waking in the middle of one: the closest cycle-friendly alarms are 6 hours 15 minutes and 7 hours 45 minutes from now (each allows about 15 minutes to drift off).

6 hours 20 minutes is 380 minutes, or 22,800 seconds. In hours and minutes that is 6 h 20 min. The alarm time updates live above, so the number you see is the actual clock time it will ring if you press the button now.

The alarm is created as a one-time alarm at the exact clock time, so you can see it in the list above, rename it, or switch the sound. Keep this tab open with the volume up; on desktop it rings from a background tab, on a phone keep the page in the foreground and the screen stays on automatically.

Prefer a countdown?

If you want to watch the time tick down rather than set a clock time, use the timer instead. Both ring with the same sounds.
