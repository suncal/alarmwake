How it works

Each card shows the current time and date in that city, whether it is day or night there, and how far ahead or behind it is from you. Times update every second and follow the 12h or 24h setting in the header. Tap the × on a card to remove it; your cities are remembered in this browser. Press the fullscreen icon for a wall of clocks that fills the screen.
