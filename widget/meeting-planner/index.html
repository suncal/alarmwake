Meeting planner

Pick up to four cities and see the hours that work for everyone. Green is a normal working hour, amber is early or late, grey is night. Click a column to set an alarm for that moment.

How to use it

The grid shows one full day in your own time, hour by hour. Each row converts that hour into the other city's local time and colours it: green for 9 AM to 5 PM, amber for 7 to 9 AM and 5 to 9 PM, grey for the night. Columns where every city is green are the best slots and are outlined. Daylight saving is handled automatically for today's date.

Click a column to see the exact time in each city and set an alarm for it; the alarm rings here at your local time. For a single conversion use the time zone converter; for the current time in many cities, the world clock.
